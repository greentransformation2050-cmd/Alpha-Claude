## Comment 1 — Roles of agencies in TA implementation (section 2.5, beneficiaries list)

> "Can we already envisage the roles of these and other relevant agencies in the TA
> implementation, beyond stakeholders in the results? … management / governance
> arrangements around TA implementation are important to define at the outset."

**Response: Yes.** A new paragraph is proposed (tracked insertion) directly after the
institutional-beneficiaries paragraph, setting out the envisaged arrangements:

- **Inter-ministerial steering committee** — convened under the authority of the
  ministry responsible for environment and climate change, with **CENAGCUE as
  technical secretariat**; quarterly meetings to provide strategic direction, validate
  deliverables and resolve interagency issues.
- **CENAGCUE + ANGUCH** — co-lead day-to-day technical coordination through a standing
  interagency technical working group guiding the L&D assessment methodology, the
  national dashboard and the SOPs.
- **ANM, DNH, ANSS** — designated data providers and system co-owners
  (hydrometeorological, water resources, health surveillance respectively), with named
  focal points and data-sharing protocols agreed at inception.
- **DGPC** — leads integration of assessment and response protocols into civil
  protection operations at regional and prefectural levels.
- **Ministry in charge of planning** — ensures alignment of outputs with the
  PDNES 2026–2030 process (see Comment 2).
- **Regional authorities and communal planning structures** — co-convene field
  consultations and host the regional technical groups trained under Component 1.
- Arrangements (decision-making, escalation, interagency engagement) to be reviewed
  and formalised with the selected implementer at the outset.

## Comment 2 — PDNES integration / Ministry of Planning & Office of the President (section 2.6)

> "This opportunity to feed results into the PNDES is an important one. Should the
> Ministry of Planning / Office of the President be included in implementation?"

**Response: Yes — include them.** A sentence is proposed (tracked insertion) at the end
of the PDNES paragraph: the ministry in charge of planning (leading PDNES 2026–2030
preparation) is directly engaged in implementation, including membership of the
steering committee, and the Office of the President is kept informed through existing
high-level coordination channels to ensure political ownership. Liz offered to add a
line herself — she can adjust or replace the proposed sentence.
