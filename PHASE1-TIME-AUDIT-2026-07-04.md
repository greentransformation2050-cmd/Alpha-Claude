## 5. COGNITIVE BIASES IDENTIFIED (Phase 1 deliverable)

1. **Intention–action gap (the headline).** Fitness: 8–10 stated, 0 executed. The strongest predictor of behavior is behavior, not stated commitment — and the behavioral signal is a flat zero.
2. **Planning fallacy / optimism bias — confirmed twice.** First live in the June 20 session ("4× a day" → corrected to 4×/week). Now confirmed structurally: believing a +1 hr/day experimental habit could be *added* onto a self-diagnosed *overload* — additive math on a subtractive problem.
3. **Deflection of subtractive decisions — now validated by data.** The June 20 pattern (substituting a list of additive priorities for naming one cut) predicted this outcome: nothing was cut, and the two additive items simply never materialized rather than displacing anything. Avoiding the cut *is* the cut — made by default, against your stated priorities.
4. **Multitasking / breadth illusion.** The CODEX portfolio spans 5 institutional roles + 4 active projects + 3 experimental + fitness. **Actual execution collapsed onto ONE project cluster (FRLD/SARITEM-2).** The portfolio is aspirational; the reality is near-monomaniacal. This is not a criticism of focus — it's evidence that the *stated* breadth is fiction and should be pruned to match reality.
5. **Survivorship of the externally-imposed.** Everything with an external counterparty and a deadline survived; everything self-authored (fitness, experiments, GT2050's own brand-building) died silently. The environment is selecting your priorities for you.
