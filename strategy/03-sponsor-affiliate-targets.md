# Real Sponsor & Affiliate Targets — Launch List

Companion to `FACELESS-CHANNEL-STRATEGY.md` Section 6. These are real, currently-operating affiliate/partner programs (verified via web search, July 2026) that fit "The Human Code" audience (psychology, self-improvement, relationships, career, mental wellness). Two of these — Skillshare and Notion — are genuinely realistic to get **approved for at zero subscribers**, because their programs are self-serve, not gatekept by follower minimums. BetterHelp and Brilliant favor an existing audience, so they're Week 3–8 targets once you have real videos to show, not Day 1. Treat this as the actual action list for the outreach step in Section 6/12, replacing the generic category placeholders.

---

## Tier 1 — Apply Day 1 (self-serve, no minimum audience required)

### 1. Skillshare Affiliate Program
- **Fit:** Online courses — direct match for "learn a skill to fix the thing this video just explained about you."
- **Commission:** 40% of the referred member's first payment (up to ~$67 per paid signup); referral window 30 days from click.
- **How to apply:** [skillshare.com/en/affiliates](https://www.skillshare.com/en/affiliates) → create a publisher account via Impact. Approval typically 3–5 business days.
- **Requirement:** At least one active channel/platform aligned with their brand (a brand-new YouTube channel + active TikTok/IG counts).
- **Action:** Apply Day 1. Once approved, add the affiliate link to every video description under a "Learn more" line, and mention it naturally in any video about self-improvement, skill-building, or the Dunning-Kruger video (#14) — a strong organic tie-in.

### 2. Notion Affiliate Program
- **Fit:** Productivity — ties naturally to Zeigarnik Effect (#30, open loops) and procrastination/temporal discounting content.
- **Commission:** 50% of the referred customer's payments for their first 12 months, uncapped, via PartnerStack.
- **How to apply:** [notion.com/affiliates](https://www.notion.com/affiliates) — fill out the application form.
- **Requirement:** A website, blog, or social presence aligned with their audience — no strict follower minimum stated.
- **Action:** Apply Day 1. Use in the Zeigarnik Effect and procrastination videos specifically — "here's the tool I use to give my brain that closure" is an honest, non-forced tie-in.

### 3. Audible / Amazon Associates (Audible Creator Program)
- **Fit:** Universal — every psychology video can reference "the book this concept comes from" as a natural next step.
- **Commission:** Standard Amazon Associates Audible payouts ($5–15 per trial/membership depending on tier); Creator Program pays up to ~$15 per trial without requiring your own website if you're an approved influencer/creator.
- **How to apply:** [audible.com/ep/affiliate-intro](https://www.audible.com/ep/affiliate-intro) and/or Amazon Associates ([affiliate-program.amazon.com](https://affiliate-program.amazon.com/)). Amazon Associates traditionally wants ~10 published posts/videos and 2 months of activity — post your first 10 Shorts before applying if the influencer-specific Creator Program isn't immediately open to you.
- **Action:** Apply once you have your first 10 videos live (roughly end of Week 1). Link the specific book/study behind each concept (e.g., Kahneman for cognitive biases, Levine & Heller for attachment styles) — genuinely useful, not just an ad.

## Tier 2 — Apply once you have real videos to show (Week 2–8)

### 4. MasterClass Affiliate Program
- **Fit:** Broad self-improvement/expert-learning audience overlap.
- **Commission:** 25% commission per sale, 30-day cookie, no minimum payout threshold.
- **How to apply:** MasterClass affiliate page → "Apply Now," basic info about your audience, approval takes a few days.
- **Action:** Apply once you have a few weeks of consistent posting to show — pure self-serve, but a channel with zero content reads as low-intent to reviewers.

### 5. BetterHelp Affiliate Program
- **Fit:** Direct niche match — mental wellness, self-understanding, trauma-response content (Fawning, Gaslighting, self-deception videos) all point naturally toward "talking to someone."
- **Commission:** $100–200 per new client referred (among the highest payouts in this list); managed primarily via Impact, also on ShareASale/Skimlinks.
- **How to apply:** Apply through Impact Radius or BetterHelp's partnerships team; they favor established mental-health/self-improvement/lifestyle audiences and review content quality, not just size.
- **Action:** Apply around Week 3–4 once you have 15–20 videos live and can point to specific, tasteful, non-sensationalized handling of sensitive topics (the trauma-response long-form video is your best portfolio piece for this application). **Important:** any mental-health-adjacent promotion must follow FTC disclosure rules and avoid clinical claims — this is also just the right thing to do given the subject matter.

### 6. Brilliant.org Sponsorship
- **Fit:** Adjacent audience (science/learning-curious), useful for cross-promotion even though it's less tightly matched than the others.
- **Deal structure:** Historically direct-negotiated (not a self-serve affiliate network) — typically "20% of the first ~200 annual-subscription signups" per integration, usually a post-roll mention.
- **How to apply:** No public self-serve application found — requires direct outreach to their sponsorships/partnerships team via their website contact channel.
- **Action:** Lower priority; revisit at Week 6–8 once you have view/retention data worth pitching (see outreach template below).

## Outreach Email Templates

### For self-serve programs (Skillshare, Notion, Audible, MasterClass) — no template needed
These are applications, not pitches. Just fill out the form honestly: describe the channel concept ("The Human Code" — 60-second psychology explainers), link your platforms, and list your (even small) posting cadence as evidence of commitment. Do this the same day you publish your first video, not before — an empty channel reads worse than a 3-video channel.

### For direct-negotiated sponsors (BetterHelp, Brilliant, or any brand without a public affiliate form)

> Subject: New psychology channel ("The Human Code") — partnership inquiry
>
> Hi [Name / Partnerships Team],
>
> I run *The Human Code* — short-form videos (YouTube Shorts, TikTok, Reels) breaking down real psychological concepts for a general audience: cognitive biases, attachment theory, behavior science, told in plain English with real sourcing, no fear-mongering or fake claims.
>
> I think there's a strong content fit with [Brand] — specifically around [name the exact concept/video, e.g., "the trauma-response video on fawning" or "the procrastination/temporal-discounting video"]. I'd love to explore a paid integration or affiliate partnership for an upcoming video.
>
> Channel snapshot: [X] videos live, [Y] total views, [Z] subscribers, posting daily. Happy to send scripts in advance so you can see tone and accuracy before committing.
>
> Is there a partnerships contact I should be speaking with, and what's your typical process for new-channel integrations?
>
> Thanks,
> [Your name] · [channel links]

**Honesty note on numbers:** never round up or imply metrics you don't have. At zero-to-small scale, lead with content quality and topical fit (link 2–3 actual scripts or published videos) rather than inflated reach claims — sponsors in this niche (especially BetterHelp) actively vet for authenticity, and a misrepresented pitch can permanently burn the relationship.
