# 30-Day Launch Calendar — "The Human Code"

Extends `FACELESS-CHANNEL-STRATEGY.md` Section 12 (Day 1–7) through a full first month. Video IDs reference `strategy/templates/idea-tracker.csv`. Scripts referenced are in `strategy/01-full-video-scripts.md` (main doc has 8, that file has the other 22). Monetization actions reference `strategy/03-sponsor-affiliate-targets.md` and `strategy/02-field-guide-product.md`.

**Cadence:** 1 Short/day on all 3 platforms simultaneously, 1 long-form video/week on YouTube (clipped into 1–2 extra Shorts from its best moments). This uses all 20 Shorts and 4 of the 10 long-form scripts in Month 1 — the remaining 6 long-form ideas queue into Month 2, which is realistic pacing for a solo creator, not a shortfall.
