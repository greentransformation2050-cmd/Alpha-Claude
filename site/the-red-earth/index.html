The Red Earth

Three minutes on the resource curse: how West African bauxite leaves as cheap ore and returns as expensive metal.

See how it unfolds

Guinea sits on roughly a quarter of the world's known bauxite. The ore ships out raw, is refined into aluminum abroad, and the value is captured where the smelters are, not where the earth is. The film names that trap, walks through its mechanism in seven movements, and ends on the question it leaves behind.

What a tonne is worth

The trap is arithmetic. The same material climbs in value at every step of refinement, and every step happens somewhere else.
